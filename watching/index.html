再生ソフト Video players for PC

Some cameras are best watched outside the browser: smoother frame rates, screenshots with one key, and no YouTube page around them. Pick your player:

  • 1. MPVSmooth 30 fps for raw rtmp streams like Yunokami-Onsen. Press S for screenshots.
  • 2. MPC-HC + StreamlinkFor YouTube streams (Bessho, Shibuya…) in a real video player.

💡 Tip: open a camera's 詳細 Details on the camera list and press 📋 Copy next to a link or raw stream URL to paste it into your player.
